# Atlas original das armaduras

Gerado com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens (image_gen). Nenhum asset de outro mod foi copiado. Arquivo final: `src/main/resources/assets/arcanecontrol/textures/armor/anime_atlas.png` (1254 × 1254). O arquivo retornado foi preservado sem edição raster. Geometria e animações foram escritas no projeto; `tools/build_armor_models.py` reproduz os modelos.

Prompt exato enviado:

```text
Create a production game texture atlas, square, 1024x1024, exactly a 4 by 4 grid of equal square material swatches with no gutters and no labels. This is a diffuse texture atlas for detailed Minecraft-adapted anime armor, NOT a concept illustration or rendered armor. Every swatch is a flat orthographic seamless hand-painted pixel-art material surface, crisp small-scale textile or metal details, no perspective, no objects, no cast shadows, no text, no borders between squares. Row 1 left to right: near-black woven uniform cloth; midnight navy woven cloth; warm ivory robe cloth with subtle folds; dark plum woven cloth. Row 2: silky silver-white hair strand texture; warm brushed gold metal; cool brushed silver metal; light peach skin. Row 3: luminous cyan-blue faceted gem surface; luminous violet faceted gem surface; deep ruby faceted gem surface; ivory robe cloth with small elegant gold embroidered motifs. Row 4: dark charcoal boot leather; pale gray soft boot leather; midnight navy cloth with subtle blue embroidery; ivory cloth with fine repeating golden trim lines. Restrained anime fantasy palette, detailed but readable at Minecraft scale. Each cell is entirely filled with its material, edge to edge. No character, mannequin, weapon, armor silhouette, garment outline or environment. Opaque background.
```
